Alongside my academic research I have written reports and analysis for policymakers and think tanks in the UK and Europe.

Recent Policy Work

Earlier Policy Work

Policy analysis of Spanish higher education policy for Fundación Alternativas: English / Spanish.

‘British Universities: Quality at the Cost of Inequality’, with Pepper Culpepper. Times Higher Education (London), February 3, 2011. Opinion piece on British higher education reform.

I served as an academic consultant to Her Majesty’s Treasury in the United Kingdom on education and immigration policy and as a consultant to the Leitch Review of Skills, an independent review established by the Chancellor of the Exchequer to examine British skills policy to 2020. The following publicly available documents reflect this work:

  • ‘Skills in the Global Economy’, released alongside the Pre-Budget Report on December 2nd, 2004.
  • ‘Leitch Review: Interim Report’, released December 2005.
  • ‘Leitch Review: Final Report’, released December 2006.
